BBC Shooting Academy

Campus of Babu Bodhraj Convent School

Equipped with state-of-the-art 10-meter indoor shooting lanes compliant with International Shooting Sport Federation (ISSF) norms. The facility houses precision electronic scoring monitors, athlete warm-up zones, calibrated lighting, and secure air-charging infrastructure.

Electronic Targets
SIUS Ascor & Meyton target monitors with instant decimal telemetry.
300 Bar Bauer Air
High-purity compressed breathing air cylinder refill station for .177 pellets.
Weapon Inspection Bay
ISSF-certified trigger pull weight, shoe sole flex, & jacket stiffness testing.
Armory & Security
Double-lock CCTV guarded overnight weapons vault for participating teams.
Transit & How to Reach
By Road (NH-34 GT Road)

Direct highway access from Delhi/NCR via Noida-Greater Noida Expressway or Eastern Peripheral Expressway (EPE Dadri Exit). Distance from Greater Noida: ~35 km (~40 min drive).

By Delhi Metro (Aqua Line)

Deboard at Pari Chowk Station or Depot Station (Noida Metro Aqua Line). Direct auto-rickshaws and cabs are readily available to BBC School Sikandrabad.

By Indian Railways

Nearest local station: Sikandrabad (SKQ) (3 km). Major railheads: Khurja Junction (25 km), Ghaziabad Jn (45 km), and New Delhi (NDLS) (65 km).

By Air

Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) - 80 km. Pre-booked taxis can reach the campus directly via DND Flyway & NH-34.

Mandatory Range Safety Rules
  • Safety Flags (CSI): All air rifles and air pistols must have a brightly colored safety flag inserted in the chamber whenever the weapon is uncased outside the firing lane.
  • Muzzle Discipline: Point weapons strictly downrange towards the targets at all times, loaded or unloaded.
  • Ear Protection: Recommended for all shooters and mandatory for range officers and scorekeeper marshals.
  • Accreditation Pass: Only registered athletes wearing their official Competitor Card and registered coaches are permitted in the firing bays.
